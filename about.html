What Is a QR Code and How to Use It

A QR code (Quick Response code) is a two-dimensional barcode that stores information such as links, text, and Wi-Fi credentials. A phone camera can scan it instantly to open or copy the encoded data.

What Is a QR Code?

QR codes are square pixel patterns designed for fast machine reading. Compared to traditional barcodes, they can hold more information and are scannable from different angles.

  • Use cases: websites, menus, product labels, event check-ins, and payments.
  • Readable by most modern phone camera apps and scanner tools.
  • Works best when printed or displayed with high color contrast.

How to Use This Generator

  1. Pick content type: URL, Text, or Wi-Fi.
  2. Enter your content in the input fields.
  3. Customize design: colors, size, corner style, and optional logo.
  4. Preview and scan-test with your phone before sharing.
  5. Download in PNG, WebP, or SVG format.

Best Practices for Reliable Scanning

  • Keep strong contrast between foreground and background.
  • Avoid very small QR sizes for printed materials.
  • Do not let the logo cover too much of the center area.
  • Leave quiet space around the QR code border.
  • Always test on multiple devices when possible.

When to Use Static vs Dynamic Links

This tool generates static QR codes, meaning the encoded data is fixed after download. Use static codes when content does not change often. For campaign tracking or editable destinations, use a dynamic QR platform that supports redirects and analytics.
